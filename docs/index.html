2026-09-09 · YouTube · Charles Hoskinson（本人のチャンネル）· 1 人語りCharles Hoskinson (own channel) · solo

OpenAI が「AI が Navier–Stokes 問題を解いた」と投稿した翌日の 18 分。本人の見解として、①その証明は数学者が ChatGPT に 1 年打ち込んだ未完成の仕事が土台になった「ように見える」、②だからクラウドの AI に入れたアイデアは自分のものでなくなり、Midnight はそのために作った、③それでも機械が検査できる証明の時代は本当に来た、の 3 点。①は本人の推測で、報道が伝える経緯とは食い違うので、両方を並べて書いた。Eighteen minutes the day after OpenAI posted that an AI had solved Navier–Stokes. Speaking for himself: the proof “looks like” it was built on a year of unfinished work a mathematician typed into ChatGPT; so ideas put into cloud AI stop being yours, and “this is why we built Midnight”; and yet the age of machine-checked proofs has genuinely arrived. The first point is his inference and differs from the reported account, so both are set out.
