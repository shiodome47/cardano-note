Santiago Carmuega of TxPipe presents Dolos, a single-binary alternative, and the problem statement MPS-0038 asking Midnight to first specify what it reads from Cardano. Then Stevan Lohja of the Midnight Foundation stops a production validator on purpose and shows his alerting agent paging him within a minute.
